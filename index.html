Thesis, financial mathematics

λ

Pricing derivatives under counterparty credit risk

My thesis extends the Black–Scholes–Merton picture to a market where the counterparty can default. Using the Burgard–Kjaer framework, I derive the risky PDE for European call options and interest-rate swaps, then solve the deterministic PDEs numerically and with physics-informed neural networks.

Burgard–Kjaer, PDE, PINN, BSM.

PDE: partial differential equation. PINN: physics-informed neural network. BSM: Black–Scholes–Merton.
